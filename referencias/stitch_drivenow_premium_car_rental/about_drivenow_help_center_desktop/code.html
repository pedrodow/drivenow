Help Center

Frequently asked questions about the Drivenow experience.

How does the booking process work? expand_more
Select your vehicle, choose your dates, and complete the digital verification. Our concierge team will handle the rest, including white-glove delivery to your location.
What are the insurance requirements? expand_more
All rentals include comprehensive coverage, though we require a valid driver's license and a security deposit. Additional premium protection plans are available during checkout.
Can I extend my rental period? expand_more
Yes, extensions are subject to availability. Please contact your dedicated concierge via the app at least 4 hours prior to your scheduled return time.
Are there mileage limits? expand_more
We offer 150 complimentary miles per day. Excess mileage is billed at a flat rate per mile depending on the specific vehicle tier.
Do you offer airport delivery? expand_more
Absolutely. We provide terminal-side delivery and pickup at major international airports within our service areas for a seamless transition.
Is there a minimum age requirement? expand_more
Renters must be at least 25 years old. For select ultra-prestige vehicles, the minimum age may be 30 years old with additional verification.
